# Triad – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 26 October 2026**

Triad ("the app") is an Android app for exploring chords, progressions and songs. It is developed and published by an individual developer under the name Sylvan Verdant Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

**Short version:** we do not collect your data in the app. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your songs stay on your device unless you choose to back them up to your own Google Drive.

## What the app stores on your device

Your songs, progressions, folders, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for Triad. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

## Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on cloud backup in Settings, Triad asks Google for permission to use a private, hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive (the `drive.appdata` permission). Triad then uploads and downloads a backup file (`triad_backup.json`) containing your songs, progressions, melodies and drum patterns, folders, custom tunings and scales, and the songs and progressions you deleted in the last 30 days (the trash), plus dated copies of it (`triad_snapshot_<time>.json`: the 5 newest and one for each of the last 7 days) so an earlier state can be restored. Older copies are deleted by the app.

- Triad cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
- The backup goes directly between your device and Google. It never passes through any server of ours, and we have no access to it: we cannot open, read or analyse your backup files.
- Triad stores the Google account you picked on your device so that it can reconnect later.
- Google processes this data under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Deleting the cloud backup in the app deletes the dated copies too.
- You can turn backup off at any time in the app. You can delete the backup data, or revoke Triad's access, in your Google Account settings under *Data & privacy → Third-party apps and services*.

Triad's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Purchases

Triad offers an optional one-time purchase ("Triad Studio"). Every financial transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- The app does not collect your card or payment details or your purchase history, and does not keep them on any server. It only asks Google Play whether Triad Studio has been bought, and remembers on your device that it is unlocked.
- Under the Google Play terms, we see in the Google Play Console only aggregated sales reports and the record of each order (order number, date, product, price and country), and use them only to handle complaints and refunds.

## App statistics from Google Play

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may show us anonymous, aggregated statistics about Triad (for example crash reports and how many devices run it). They do not identify you.

## If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you and keep them no longer than needed for that.

## Sharing and exporting

When you export a song as audio or text and share it, the file is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not receive anything.

## Permissions

- **Internet** – used only for the optional Google Drive backup and for Google Play Billing.
- **Notifications and foreground playback** – show what is playing (the song's or progression's name) with Pause, Play and Stop in the notification and on the lock screen, and keep it playing with the screen off. Android may also show the name on devices you connect, such as a car or headphones. Nothing is sent to us.

Triad does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Third parties

The only third-party services involved are Google Drive (if you enable backup) and Google Play (billing). Triad contains no advertising or analytics software and does not sell or share personal data.

## Children

Triad is a general-audience music tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete, apart from any emails you sent us. Your data is on your device and, if you enabled backup, in your own Google Drive, where you control it. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the one where you live.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
